Board Roamer for Android

Version 1.1.1 · 299 KB · Android 10 or newer

The file is a zip holding BoardRoamer.apk. Extract it in the Files app, then open the APK.

Install on your S24+

  1. Tap the button, accept the save, then open the zip in the Files app and choose Extract. Open BoardRoamer.apk from the extracted folder.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then go back and tap Install.
  3. If Play Protect says the app is unrecognized, choose Install anyway. The app is signed with a private key made for this project.
  4. Open Board Roamer. Saved images go to Downloads/Board Roamer with their tags and artists embedded.

Worth trying first

  • Settings → Sync pairs it with the desktop app over Tailscale or Wi-Fi.
  • Search a tag, then long-press a thumbnail for its menu.
  • In the viewer: swipe to move, pinch or double-tap to zoom, drag up for details.
  • Press the Back button to close layers one at a time.
  • Settings has a Phone and touch section for columns, haptics and screen lock.
